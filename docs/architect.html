What the explorer preserves

One accuracy limit, three system decisions.

1

Protect local SNDR

Device conductance, variation, wire parasitics, sensing mismatch, and ADC behavior determine the quality of each bank-level dot product.

2

Replicate small banks

A workload dimension larger than N is partitioned across banks. More physical banks reduce mapping waves without increasing the local analog dimension.

3

Reduce digitally

The parallel pipeline digitizes local outputs before combining partial sums, keeping system scaling separate from the bank-level analog accuracy estimate.
